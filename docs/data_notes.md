## ⚠️ Narratives removed from the source (Sep 2026)

CFPB release notes (https://cfpb.github.io/api/ccdb/release-notes.html, checked 2026-09-29):

- **Release 24 (Sep 2026):** "Consumers' complaint narratives and complaint data visualizations have
  been removed from the database."
- **Release 23 (Jul 2026):** filtered exports capped at 100,000 complaints (CSV); JSON export discontinued.
- **Release 22 (Jun 2026):** `Consumer disputed` and `Consumer consent provided` removed from exports.

The current bulk file therefore has **15 columns, not the 18 listed in spec §2**. Missing:
`Consumer complaint narrative`, `Consumer consent provided?`, `Consumer disputed?`. The field
reference (https://cfpb.github.io/api/ccdb/fields.html) lists the same 15 fields.

**Impact:** spec Phase 2 (narrative enrichment, AI Search over narratives, root-cause assistant)
and the gold metric "% with narrative" can't be built on current CFPB data as specified. Milestone 1
is unaffected. Needs a spec decision before Milestone 4.

### Findings and design implications (hand-written; snapshot 2026-09-29)

1. **Three taxonomy generations.** Product labels switch over around **2017-04-24** and
   **2023-08-24/25**, with a few days of overlap at each cutover (for example `Checking or savings
   account` first appears 2017-04-21). Map **by label, not by date**. Some labels persist across
   generations (`Credit card`, `Mortgage`, `Debt collection`, `Student loan`, `Prepaid card`), but
   `Credit card` and `Prepaid card` were merged between 2017 and 2023 as `Credit card or prepaid
   card`. There are 2,645 product/sub-product/issue/sub-issue combinations, so taxonomy v1 maps
   **product and issue** and leaves sub-issue for later. Input: `profile/2026-09-29/taxonomy_combinations.csv`.
2. **The three credit bureaus are 78% of all complaints** (TransUnion 27.6%, Equifax 26.6%,
   Experian 24.2%), and `Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports` alone is 69% of rows.
   Peer groups and spike ranking must separate bureaus from banks, or the bureaus drown out every
   bank-level signal (M2/M3).
3. **Volume grew about 7× from 2022 to 2025** (0.8M to 5.4M per year), driven by credit reporting.
   Spike baselines must be relative (the robust z over a trailing window in spec §4.4 handles this),
   and all-history averages would mislead.
4. **3.4% of rows are `In progress`** and will get a final company response later. That confirms
   the AUTO CDC upsert path (spec §4.2), since later files revise existing complaint IDs.
5. **State:** 99.64% are USPS codes. The one non-code value, `UNITED STATES MINOR OUTLYING ISLANDS`
   (1,516 rows), maps to ISO `UM`. 62,969 rows (0.35%) have no state. All 62 USPS codes occur.
6. **Legacy company-response labels** (`Closed`, `Closed with relief`, `Closed without relief`) occur
   alongside today's `Closed with monetary relief` / `non-monetary relief`. The gold "% monetary
   relief" metric needs an explicit rule for them (M2).
7. **ZIP codes are masked for 7%** of rows (`123XX` 6.25%, `XXXXX` 0.77%), so geographic analysis
   stays at state level (ZIP3 is possible for about 99%).
8. **`Tags`** has 3 values (`Servicemember`, `Older American`, both) on 4.5% of rows. It's the only
   vulnerable-consumer signal left now that narratives are gone.
9. **7,050 rows (0.04%) were sent to the company before they were received.** It's a source quirk;
   we keep the rows and add no expectation.
10. **Reporting lag can't be estimated from one snapshot.** September 2026 (29 days) has 546,963
    rows against about 650k in full months, but partial-month and lag effects can't be separated.
    The lag curve in spec §4.4 needs first-seen dates, which start accumulating in bronze with the
    first daily delta. Until then, fall back to excluding the latest 2 weeks.

| Organizations | 4,011 top-level (holding company, or standalone bank) | computed |
| **`ASSET` unit** | **thousands of USD: inferred, not stated in the field definition.** In dollars the largest bank would be $4.1B and the smallest $3.5k, both impossible. `download_fdic.check_asset_magnitude` fails the download if the largest bank isn't $1T–$10T when read in thousands | magnitude + Call Report convention |

Holding-company assets = sum of member banks' `ASSET` (organizations by tier: 17 above $250B,
15 at $100–250B, 121 at $10–100B, 3,858 below $10B). Largest: JPMORGAN CHASE&CO $4,091B, BANK OF
AMERICA CORP $2,669B, CITIGROUP INC $1,976B, WELLS FARGO&COMPANY $1,919B.

**Limitation:** only active institutions, at one quarter-end. Complaints against banks that have
since merged or closed get no FDIC match (they fall into the non-bank or unmatched groups in step 3).
